# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (confirmed by the user). No framework, no build step. GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis and the web fonts are vendored in the repo (no CDN, no third-party font host).

## Users

Women (and some men) living in or around Brest who want their nails done, a facial or body treatment, hair removal, brows/lashes or make-up, in a calm and welcoming place. They arrive from Instagram or Google, mostly on a phone, and decide to book (Planity) or call. Students are a named audience (10 % discount on presentation of a student card).

## Product Purpose

Angel's Concept is a beauty institute at 28 Quai de la Douane, 29200 Brest (sea view, per client reviews). The site's job: make a visitor feel the place, understand the offer and prices, and book an appointment (Planity) or call 07 44 83 28 08.

## Positioning

A small, warm "cocoon" institute that combines advanced nail work (gel, American extensions, level 1 to 3 nail art with chrome, 3D and gold leaf) with face and body care, including the Bloomea "La Fontaine" device (macro-exfoliation + micro-vibrations + light therapy).

## Operating Context

- Address: 28 Quai de la Douane, 29200 Brest. Phone: 07 44 83 28 08.
- Hours: Mon 14:00-19:30; Tue-Fri 09:30-19:30; Sat 10:00-17:00; Sun closed (Toussaint note). "Hours may change."
- Booking: https://www.planity.com/angels-concept-29200-brest (some services cannot be booked online: call instead).
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/angels.concept/
- Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/TUiWiv3PrMDAwP1W9

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: French only.
- Price list was pasted from OCR'd text; several prices and most nail durations are ambiguous. Best readings are displayed and flagged for the owner to confirm (see the hand-off notes). Uncertain durations are omitted.
- Services not bookable online: gommage gourmand and gommage + enveloppement.
- The institute does not refill or remove work done by another institute (except by dedicated booking).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Angel's Concept. Tone: convivial, chaleureux, angélique.
- Colors imposed by the client: #ECE3D4 and #F8FBA4.

## Evidence on Hand

- 5 client-supplied photos (nail art x3, team photo of four, face treatment) in `assets/img/`. The face-treatment photo is very small (195x325).
- 5 real client reviews, quoted verbatim (names as supplied). No rating figure is known: none is shown.
- No verified figures for years of activity or number of clients: none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim comes from supplied facts; illustrative material is never presented as real.
2. Booking and calling are reachable from every screen within one gesture.
3. The place is warm and generous; the information (prices, hours, address) stays plain and fast to find.
4. Motion is rich but never gets between the visitor and the price list or the phone number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast on the light palette, keyboard navigation, visible focus, alt text on every photo, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, tap targets of 44 px or more on mobile.
