# Surface brief: index.html (Angel's Concept, one-page site)

Scope and mode: Persuade. One-page marketing site for a beauty institute in Brest; the visitor must book (Planity) or call.

Audience, job, action, proof, constraints: see PRODUCT.md. Proof = 5 real client reviews (verbatim), 5 client photos, supplied price list. No invented figures. Static HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis vendored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the appointment book of a small angelic institute, open on the reception desk: paper, highlighter, date stamps, perforated tickets, index tabs, washi tape. Booking is the act the whole page performs. It refuses the cream-serif-pastel salon template with a centred headline over a photo.

OWN-WORLD: Agenda paper #ECE3D4 as ground. Highlighter #F8FBA4 used as real marker swipes and as drenched full fields. Warm ink for type, deep olive "back cover" for dark sections, thin gold-leaf rings. Components: index tabs, ticket stubs with notches, circular date stamps (the halo, rotating), tape strips holding arch-topped photos, dotted-leader price rows.

STORY: A visitor sees a warm place and ambitious nail work, understands the range and prices at a glance, trusts the real reviews, and books on Planity or calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-weighted giant "Angel's Concept" over two lines, a highlighter swipe drawing behind "Concept", a rotating circular stamp (the halo) perched over the A. Three arch photos taped at different depths on the right with multi-layer parallax. Ticket-stub primary action "Prendre rendez-vous" bottom-left with a live "open today until 19:30" line. Nav on top, phone reachable.
